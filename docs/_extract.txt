 serialisation

serials to be accounted for exactly 
 — 
and the reward for that rigour is a recall capability measured in 
hours. The second is 
 the compliance gates
 : they are not friction to be optimised away under 
deadline pressure, they are the licence to trade. Built to this specification, and never descoping what 
is marked [COMPLIANCE]
 , Phoenix Vapers will operate the most trustworthy, most traceable, and most 
automated commerce platform in the United Kingdom vaping market.  
Anything marked 
 [OPEN]  
is an explicit invitation to decide, not an omission to paper over. Bring those 
decisions to the platform owner and the compliance owner, record them as ADRs, and proceed.
